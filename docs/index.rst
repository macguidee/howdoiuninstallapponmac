How Do I Uninstall Apps on Mac?
================================

Mac is a common task, but the best method depends on where the app came from and how completely you want to remove it.

While dragging an app to the Trash is the standard method, some apps leave behind support files that continue to take up storage space. This guide covers the different ways to uninstall apps on your Mac.

What You Need Before Starting
=============================

Before you begin uninstalling apps, keep the following points in mind:

- Uninstalling an app does not remove any documents or other files you created with that app . However, you may need the app to open those files later.
- Uninstalling an app does not cancel any subscription you purchased through that app . You must cancel subscriptions separately, such as through the App Store or your Apple ID account settings.
- If the app is currently open, quit the app before you try to uninstall it .

Method 1: Using Finder (Standard Method)
========================================

The most common way to uninstall an app on a Mac is through Finder. Apple recommends this approach for most apps .

Step 1: Open the Applications Folder
------------------------------------

Open a Finder window. In the sidebar, click Applications. Alternatively, you can select Go from the menu bar and choose Applications.

Step 2: Locate the App
----------------------

Find the app you want to uninstall. You can also use Spotlight to search for the app, then hold the Command key and double-click the app in the search results to reveal it in Finder .

Step 3: Move the App to Trash
-----------------------------

Drag the app icon to the Trash in your Dock. Alternatively, you can select the app and choose File > Move to Trash from the Finder menu bar .

Step 4: Enter Administrator Credentials
---------------------------------------

If prompted, enter the name and password of an administrator account on your Mac. This is usually the same name and password you use to log in .

Step 5: Empty the Trash
-----------------------

To permanently delete the app and free up its storage space, right-click the Trash icon and select Empty Trash. You can also choose Finder > Empty Trash from the menu bar .

Method 2: Using Launchpad (for App Store Apps)
==============================================

Apps downloaded from the App Store can be easily removed using Launchpad .

Step 1: Open Launchpad
----------------------

Click the Launchpad icon in your Dock.

Step 2: Enter Jiggle Mode
-------------------------

Click and hold any app icon until all the icons start shaking.

Step 3: Click the Delete Button
-------------------------------

Click the X button that appears in the corner of the app you want to uninstall.

Step 4: Confirm Deletion
------------------------

Click Delete to confirm. If an app does not show an X button, it likely came from outside the App Store and you will need to use Finder to uninstall it .

Method 3: Using System Settings
===============================

You can also uninstall apps through System Settings, which provides a convenient way to view and remove applications while seeing their sizes .

Step 1: Open System Settings
----------------------------

Go to the Apple menu and select System Settings.

Step 2: Go to Storage
---------------------

Select General, then click Storage.

Step 3: View Applications
-------------------------

Click the information icon next to Applications to see a list of all installed apps sorted by size.

Step 4: Delete the App
----------------------

Select the app you want to remove and click Delete. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This method works for both App Store and third-party apps .

Method 4: Using a Dedicated Uninstaller
=======================================

Some apps, especially more complex ones like Adobe Creative Cloud or antivirus software, include their own uninstaller .

Step 1: Check for an Uninstaller
--------------------------------

Look in the app's folder inside the Applications folder for a file named Uninstall or Uninstaller .

Step 2: Run the Uninstaller
---------------------------

Double-click the uninstaller and follow the on-screen instructions. This is the best way to remove the app because the uninstaller knows where all associated files, login items, and extensions are stored .

Some apps may also offer an uninstall option as a menu item or setting within the app itself .

Method 5: Using Third-Party Uninstaller Apps
============================================

For a more thorough cleanup, you can use third-party uninstaller tools. These apps scan for support files, caches, and hidden folders after the main app is removed .

Popular options include AppCleaner, CleanMyMac X, and AppZapper . These tools can save time by automatically finding and removing leftover files that manual deletion may miss .

What About Leftover Files?
==========================

Dragging an app to the Trash often leaves behind support files, preferences, caches, and logs in the Library folder . These files are usually small, but they can accumulate over time.

If you want to remove all traces of an app, you can manually check the following folders inside your user Library folder. To access the Library folder, open Finder, select Go from the menu bar, hold the Option key, and choose Library .

Check these folders for files or folders with the app name or developer name:

- ~/Library/Application Support 
- ~/Library/Caches 
- ~/Library/Preferences 
- ~/Library/Containers 
- ~/Library/Logs 

Move any related files to the Trash and empty it. Be careful not to delete files for other apps or system components.

Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

Why can't I delete some apps on my Mac?
---------------------------------------
You cannot use Finder to delete apps that are required by macOS . This includes built-in apps like Mail, Music, Books, Notes, Podcasts, Maps, News, and Stocks . These apps are part of the operating system.

What if my Mac says the app is in use?
--------------------------------------
If you receive an error that the app is in use, make sure the app is not currently open . If necessary, restart your Mac or start up in Safe Mode and try again .

Can I reinstall an app after uninstalling it?
---------------------------------------------
If you uninstall an app, you can reinstall it later. For App Store apps, you can download them again from the Purchased section in the App Store . For third-party apps, you will need to download the installer again from the developer's website.

Conclusion
==========

Uninstalling apps on a Mac is a straightforward process, but the right method depends on where the app came from and how completely you want to remove it. For most apps, dragging them to the Trash and emptying it is sufficient. Apps from the App Store can be removed through Launchpad or the App Store. Some complex apps include dedicated uninstallers for a cleaner removal. If you want to ensure all leftover files are removed, you can manually check Library folders or use a third-party uninstaller tool. By following these methods, you can manage your storage effectively and keep your Mac organized.
